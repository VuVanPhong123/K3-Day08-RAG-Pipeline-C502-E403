## How to apply

### 1. Find your program

The first step to joining RMIT is to find a program that suits you best. Each RMIT program page includes useful information including career prospects, entry eligibility and fees. Search for an [undergraduate program](/study-at-rmit/undergraduate-programs) in a study area that interests you.

### 2. Check your eligibility

* If your level of English is below IELTS (Academic) 6.5 (no band under 6.0), you may have to consider studying an [English for University program](/study-at-rmit/pathway-programs/english-for-university) first

### 3. Prepare your documents

To avoid delays in admission processing, please ensure you have all the necessary supporting documents ready to include in your application.

### False documents and misleading information

Creating, submitting or using fraudulent documents constitutes a breach of the RMIT Student Charter and may be deemed misconduct under the Student Conduct Policy.

Students who submit fraudulent documents may face a range of consequences, from failing an assessment task through to permanent expulsion or revocation of qualification/s.

Find out more about [document fraud](/students/my-studies/false-documents-misleading-information "information for students on fraudulent documents"), how you can avoid it and where to access free support.

### 4. Submit your application

Fill out the online application form (registration required) and attach all relevant supporting documents (refer to the Application checklist to see what documents are required).

Once your application is complete, follow the prompts and submit your application.

If you are unable to apply via our online portal, please contact our Student Recruitment team.

The closing dates for applications are published on [important dates for students](/study-at-rmit/important-dates-for-students). Please note, RMIT’s offer is subject to the availability of places in each program and applications to competitive programs may close when program quotas fill before published deadlines. As such, applicants are encouraged to apply as soon as possible to avoid disappointment.

Your application will be assessed in line with RMIT’s policies and procedures. If you are successful, you will receive an offer letter online. Follow the prompts to accept your offer via the online application platform.

RMIT will normally advise you on the outcome of your application within 10 business days. If you don't hear back within the time frame above please contact our Student Recruitment team.

### Didn’t get an offer?

If you are dissatisfied with the outcome of your application for admission, you may seek a formal review of a selection decision in accordance with RMIT’s Selection Procedure.

Our team is here to support you and answer any questions you have about studying at RMIT or preparing your application.
